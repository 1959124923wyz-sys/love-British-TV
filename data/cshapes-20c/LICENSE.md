# CShapes-derived historical boundary data

The GeoJSON files in this directory are adaptations of CShapes 2.0, obtained through the BorderDrift repository's simplified CShapes import. They are **not relicensed under the general project license**.

- Original authors: Guy Schvitz, Luc Girardin, Seraina Rüegger, Nils B. Weidmann, Lars-Erik Cederman and Kristian Skrede Gleditsch. *Mapping the International System, 1886–2019: The CShapes 2.0 Dataset*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/00220027211013563
- Original data: https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/
- Intermediate adaptation: https://github.com/borderdrift-org/borderdrift/tree/main/data/imports/cshapes-2-0
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Attribution, noncommercial use and share-alike conditions apply to these data adaptations.
- Adaptation: select British-related historical footprints, attach Chinese descriptions and year-end intervals, preserve intermediate coordinates, distinguish legal administration and local government, and correct independently sourced dated transitions. Each imported feature retains its upstream Git blob identity and source coding. Approximate outlines are dashed and excluded from area statistics.
- These datasets are candidate editorial-review material. Verify intended use complies with the noncommercial license before public deployment. No endorsement is implied. The separate atlas source data outside this directory retain their respective licenses.

The official dataset and original source coding should be consulted for discrepancies. The generalized polygons are not a claim of precise military occupation or a cadastral survey.
