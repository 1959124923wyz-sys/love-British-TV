# Robert Aske 1537 York Castle execution: editorial verification
Historical role: 1536 "Pilgrimage of Grace" armed protest is an existing event (1530S-E011) attributed to rebels; **1537 royal execution is a separate new event**, not duplicated resistance.
English Heritage states Aske was hanged at the castle dungeon height in 1537, citing Tudor Letters and Papers. UK National Archives' education source says Aske was arrested and hanged in chains at York Castle after renewed fighting. A documented historical study gives 12 July 1537.
Current English Heritage Clifford's Tower visitor guide gives 53.955856,-1.079992, and City of York HER confirms monument grid location SE6046 5146. These mark the surviving historic keep, **not a proven spot of the 1537 gallows**. No new English controlled polygon or inferred casualty count.
